02 Round two · unique territory

Four names nobody else in dev-ed owns

Round one was too safe — Corpus, Recall and Primer are words the AI-tooling crowd has already worn smooth. These four come from older vocabularies: manuscript production, formal proof, and the idea that a lesson should be reduced rather than padded. Each one is a real word, each mark is built from one geometric idea, and each holds together at 16px.

2a

/ˈsuː.trə/ · noun

Literally thread — and the name for the world's oldest text-first pedagogy: teaching compressed into lines so dense they had to be unpacked slowly. Your product's exact thesis, 2,500 years early. Nobody in developer education is using it.

/ˈkwaɪ.ə/ · noun

A bookbinder's word: the gathering of folded sheets that becomes one section of a book. A quire is exactly what a course is — a bundle of pages, complete in itself, bound into something larger. Rare, short, and the Q makes it a gift to draw.
